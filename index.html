HOW TO GOVERN

ON FOOT

WASD / left stick move · Drag / right side look · Space jump · E interact · F enter or exit a vehicle.

Walk into the White House to enter the Oval Office. The Resolute Desk opens the government dashboard.

VEHICLES

Approach The Beast or Marine One. On mobile, the gold button boards. Cars use the stick for throttle and steering; the helicopter uses ↑ / ↓ to climb and descend.

You can fly to any nation from the world map or by boarding Air Force One at Andrews.

GOVERNING

Oval opens the Situation Room: policies, diplomacy, military operations, intelligence, nuclear posture, elections, and media.

Time runs while you explore. Pause it any time from the top-left speed bar.

THE WORLD

Every nation has a relationship, economy, military, and memory. Sanctions, strikes, alliances, and state visits all change how the world reacts.

Dynamic events interrupt the clock and demand a decision. There are no scripted missions — only consequences.

A NOTE ON REALITY

This is a stylized, fictional simulation. Leaders, events, and outcomes are invented. The world is compressed for play. Nothing here is policy advice, and no real person is depicted.

Built with Three.js and a lot of coffee. Everything you see is procedural.
